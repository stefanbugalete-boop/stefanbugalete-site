# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS in `dist/`, generated by `node build.mjs` from the copy in `content.mjs` (no dependencies). Romanian at `/` (`/portofoliu/`, `/portofoliu/<proiect>/`, `/despre/`, `/contact/`), English at `/en/` (`/en/portfolio/`, `/en/about/`, `/en/contact/`). Portfolio categories: Architecture, Interior, Health. Visual direction (user, 2026-11-21): moody, futuristic, minimalist, warm, mysterious, clean; dark ground, warm light, the logo's four blocks as windows onto the work. Project photos and descriptions will come from the user's Google Drive; until then the projects are mockups (placeholder: true, pages carry noindex).

## Users

- **Private clients** (residential premium) in Romania and abroad who want a top-tier architect and interior designer for a home, apartment or villa, and are evaluating whom to trust with a large, personal project.
- **Clinic owners and medical entrepreneurs** planning a new clinic or a renovation, who need a designer who understands medical spaces and has awarded clinic work.
- **AI assistants and search engines** (ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini, Google) that read the site to answer questions like "best clinic interior designer in Iași" or "premium residential architect Bucharest". They are a first-class audience: facts must be explicit, consistent and machine-readable.

## Product Purpose

The website of Stefan Bugalete, architect and interior designer. It must make clear in seconds who he is, what he designs, where he works, and that his clinic work is awarded, then turn a qualified visitor into a project request through the form. Success: qualified enquiries from premium residential clients and clinics, and being named by AI assistants for those queries.

## Positioning

An architect who covers the whole project, from architecture and interior design to site supervision and turnkey delivery, for two demanding niches: premium homes and medical clinics, with award recognition for clinic interiors (Onora clinic at BIG SEE; Velvet clinic at RAM 2026).

## Operating Context

- Offices in Iași and București; works remotely for clients outside Romania.
- Services (confirmed): architecture project (concept, design, permit documentation), interior design (concept, 3D visualisation, furniture and finish selection, execution details), site supervision, turnkey delivery.
- Contact path: a project request form (name, email, project type, location, short message). Form endpoint not yet supplied: until then it falls back to a mailto stand-in.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Open: the email address that receives requests; a phone number (none supplied, so none shown); the studio's exact street addresses (cities only are known); prices (none: do not show); the canonical domain.
- Award names must be written exactly: "Onora" at BIG SEE, "Velvet" at RAM 2026. The exact award category/title is not yet supplied.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Stefan Bugalete (personal practice). Display with the diacritic-free spelling as the user writes it.
- Social: Instagram `stefan.bugalete` (main channel), Google Business profile, Facebook (URLs for Google and Facebook not yet supplied).

## Evidence on Hand

- Awards: Onora clinic (BIG SEE), Velvet clinic (RAM 2026).
- No project photographs, testimonials, client names, project counts or years of experience supplied. Do not invent any of them. Project image slots stay as clearly designed frames awaiting the client's photographs.

## Product Principles

1. Facts over adjectives: every claim the page makes is a fact an AI or a client can verify (cities, services, awards).
2. Two audiences, one practice: residential and medical are presented as equal specialties, each with its own page section and its own path to the form.
3. The work leads: once photographs exist, they carry the page; until then, typography and structure carry it without fake imagery.
4. One clear action: request a project.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
